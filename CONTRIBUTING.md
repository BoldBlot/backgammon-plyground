# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Backgammon PlyGround.

**Code contributions are not accepted.** Please don't open pull requests or send patches: they are closed
without review. This keeps the project's own code under a single copyright holder.

You are welcome to:

- report a bug or suggest an idea by opening an issue (a position's XGID, the app version and what you
  expected help a lot);
- fork the project and change it for yourself under the terms of its licence (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and
  the Licence section of the [README](README.md)).

The third-party components in `engine/` and the typeface keep their own licences and authors, as listed in
the README.
